**You can use the command below to summon fireworks:**
- /afw summon [explosion effect] [color]

> [!IMPORTANT]
># AuroraFireworks Tutorial

># 💥 Explosion Effects
- Aurora - Creates a small sea of stars above the player's head, lasting for 10 seconds.
- AtomicBomb - Creates an explosion effect in front of the player, lasting for 10 seconds.
- circle - Creates a magic circle explosion effect beneath the player's feet, lasting for 10 seconds.
- SpellAttack - Creates a moving spell attack in front of the player; the explosion effect lasts for 10 seconds.

># 🟢 Colors

- orange
- yellow
- green
- cyan
- blue
- purple
- black
- white
- gray
- Corresponding English names
